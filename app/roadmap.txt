  ------------ Profiles Analytic module ------------

  What useful information can be extracted?
  From current fields (user_id, username, rank_title, status, messages, years_on_site, source_prefix) I can derive:

  Direct signals:

  1. Message count — bots/trolls often have either 0 (sleeper accounts) or suspiciously high counts
  2. Years on site - accounts created close to the election period are suspicious.
  3. Rank title — maps to activity tier; mismatches between rank and years are a red flag
  4. Status (online/offline) — real-time presence pattern.

  Derived/computed signals:

  1. Messages per year = messages / years_on_site — unnaturally high ratios suggest coordinated burst activity
  2. Account age bucket — group into pre-2019, 2019–2020, post-2020 to isolate election-era registrations
  3. Zero-message accounts — large clusters of 0-message accounts registered in the same period are a classic bot army signature
  4. Username pattern analysis — sequential IDs with similar naming patterns (Angel-A_2012, Angel-B_2012…)
  5. Registration date clustering — histogram of account creation by month; spikes near Aug–Nov 2020 are highly meaningful
  6. Dormancy ratio — accounts registered but never posting
  7. Activities in: Forum topics/Forum messages/Baracholka messages/Catalogue messages/Car messages/ Bike messages.
